## Recording Tips

1. **Start broad** — show the workspace canvas and panels first, then zoom into each one
2. **Show the AI doing things** — don't just describe panels, show the AI using them
3. **Use real scenarios** — "schedule a meeting", "create an invoice", "search my notes"
4. **Highlight the local aspect** — repeatedly emphasize no cloud, no account, everything on your Mac
5. **Show the multi-panel layout** — demonstrate the workspace flexibility early
6. **End with privacy** — leave viewers with the "your data never leaves your Mac" message
